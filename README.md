# Seamwillow 1.0 TEST
Native Java/Canvas Android 8+ rectangle-pair area-sum puzzle.

Fence the full grid into filled rectangles or squares. Each numbered seam must be a wall between two different rectangles. Its number is the SUM of the full areas of those two rectangles. Rectangles without clues and equal-area neighbors are allowed. No extra wall may remain inside one rectangle. Twelve distinct 4x3 clue boards have twelve different unique partitions.

Saved seam palette and cell guides, GG007 icon/menu/site/privacy, unlimited undo/reset. Offline, no ads, analytics, account or requested permissions. Settings and completed-puzzle marks stay on device. External studio link opens browser.

Independent Python exact cover packs axis-aligned rectangles and records all 3164 tilings with their original pair-area sums. A Java lookup oracle agrees with production floodfill and bounding-box rectangle checks on all 131072 border masks per board. Unique solution, bounds, solve, undo and replay pass. Adapter navigation, saved settings, privacy, edge taps, next, levels and persistence pass. Actual drawing pixels inspected, not phone screenshots. Phone runtime, performance and browser handoff QA incomplete. Signed TEST only, not final or Play published.

Current 803-entry registry, 116 partial recovered README records and exact Bendmaple cumulative stage found no rectangle-pair area-sum match. Shared rectangle fencing with Mosaicmeadow, Sumhaven and Willowcourt is disclosed. Here clues lie on SEAMS and constrain the sum of TWO adjacent full room areas, instead of a single room area, width plus height, or clue-cell fenced-side count. Squarehaven sums adjacent dark square areas from a fixed light cell, without partitioning the grid into rectangles. Paircourt seam circles add adjacent cell digits, not whole regions. This is the rectangle-pair variant sourced from the WPC2016 example, not the arbitrary-region Wafusuma variant with unequal neighboring areas. Known puzzle family, not a global invention. Partial recovered corpus, not ALL-local. Rules: https://www.logic-puzzles.ropeko.ch/php/db/puzzle.php?id=472 .
